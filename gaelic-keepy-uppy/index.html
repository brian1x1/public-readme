Gaelic Keepy-Uppy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Gaelic Keepy-Uppy (the “App”) handles information. The App is a one-time paid, offline-first game with no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. The developer operates no custom servers and does not sell any data.

Information the App does not collect

The developer does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you, and does not use any advertising or analytics software development kits. The App does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game data locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This includes:

  • personal best and today’s best scores, and your daily play streak;
  • coins earned, owned and selected ball skins, and any banked extra lives;
  • lifetime statistics (such as games played, kicks, and hoops);
  • your settings, including sound, haptics, daily-reminder, Casual-mode, and Black-&-White (colour) preferences.

This information stays on your device (except where you enable the optional Google Play Games Services features below) and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you choose to sign in, the App uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in, leaderboards, achievements, and Cloud Save of your best scores. When you use these optional features, Google may process a Play Games player identifier and your saved scores on Google’s infrastructure. This data is handled by Google under its own terms, not by the developer. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games Services information for details. Sign-in is optional — if you skip it, the App runs fully offline and your scores are kept only on your device.

Notifications (optional)

The App can send you a local daily reminder to keep your play streak going. These reminders are generated on your device and send no information off it. They are optional: you can turn them off in the App’s Settings, or deny the notification permission when prompted (Android 13 and later).

Sharing your score

The game-over screen lets you share your score as an image and text. This is only ever started by you. When you tap share, the App hands the score card to your device’s standard share sheet, and you choose which app to send it to. The developer does not transmit or receive anything you share; once shared, the content is handled by whichever app or service you select.

Permissions

The App requests only:

  • Network access — used to sync with Google Play Games Services when you are signed in, and to hand a shared score to another app you choose. The App runs fully offline without it.
  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily streak reminder.

No other sensitive device permissions are requested.

Data retention & deletion

Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. If you signed in to Google Play Games Services, any Cloud Save (Saved Games) data associated with the App can be viewed and deleted through the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or per-game Saved Games management) or your Google Account. That data is held by Google, not the developer.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the developer contact details shown on the App’s Google Play listing.
